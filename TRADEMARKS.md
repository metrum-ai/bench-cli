subject to this policy and applicable law.

## 2. Permitted use without permission

Without a separate written trademark license, you may:

1. **Refer to the software** - Use the marks in factual, non-misleading
   references to the open-source software (for example: "runs Metrum AI Bench
   CLI" or "compatible with Metrum AI Bench CLI").
2. **"Measured with Metrum AI Bench CLI"** - Use that phrase **only** when a
   full run manifest, including the **SUT block**, is published alongside the
   claim. See
   [docs/RESULTS_PUBLICATION_POLICY.md](docs/RESULTS_PUBLICATION_POLICY.md).
3. **Unmodified binaries with NOTICE** - Redistribute unmodified official
   binaries that retain the accompanying `NOTICE` and license files.

These uses must not imply endorsement, certification, or affiliation beyond
factual reference.

## 3. Requires written agreement

The following require a prior written agreement with Metrum AI, Inc.:

- Claims that a product, system, or result is **validated**, **certified**, or
  otherwise **endorsed** by Metrum AI
- Use of any Metrum AI mark as (or in) a **product name**
- Use of Metrum AI **logos**, wordmarks as logos, or other brand assets

Contact: [opensource@metrum.ai](mailto:opensource@metrum.ai).

## 4. Forks

Forks of this software **must not** use the Metrum AI marks in the fork's name,
binary names, package names, or marketing as if the fork were an official
Metrum AI release.

Suggested attribution form: **"based on Metrum AI Bench CLI"**.

## 5. Results publication

Publication of measurements that name Metrum AI Bench CLI, and use of
"Measured with Metrum AI Bench CLI," is governed by
[docs/RESULTS_PUBLICATION_POLICY.md](docs/RESULTS_PUBLICATION_POLICY.md).
